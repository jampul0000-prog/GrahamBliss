FAQ

Questions we
get asked most

Can't find your answer here? Send us a note on the contact page.

How do I place an order?

Add boxes to your cart from the Products page, review the cart, then check out. You'll need an account to complete checkout.

What payment methods do you accept?

GCash, bank transfer, and cash on delivery for orders within Sariaya and nearby towns.

How long does delivery take?

Same-day delivery within Sariaya, and 1–2 days for the rest of Quezon province. We pack with ice to keep the filling firm.

How long do the graham balls stay fresh?

Up to 3 days at room temperature, or 7 days chilled. We recommend chilling them as soon as they arrive.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Message us within 24 hours of delivery with a photo of the box and we'll replace it or refund the affected items.

Can I customize a box with mixed flavors?

Yes — mention the mix you'd like in your order notes at checkout, or message us directly for bulk or party orders.

Do you cater for events or bulk pasalubong orders?

Yes, we take bulk orders with a few days' notice. Reach out through the contact page with your date and quantity.
