Mission

Make every bite delicious, affordable, and memorable

Graham Bliss aims to provide delicious, affordable, and enjoyable Graham Balls with different flavors that suit the preferences of our customers. We aim to promote our product through an attractive and user-friendly website where customers can easily learn about our flavors, prices, features, and promotions. Through creativity, quality, and technology, we strive to build a memorable brand while developing our skills in entrepreneurship, branding, and digital marketing.

Vision

Grow a recognizable student-made brand

To become a simple and recognizable student-made snack brand that brings affordable, colorful, and delicious Graham Balls to students and other customers through both food products and digital marketing.

Our story

From a simple idea to a sweet student-made treat

Graham Bliss started with a simple idea: to create a delicious and affordable snack that students and other customers could enjoy. What began as a small project among four students grew into a product we were proud to share with others. We experimented with different flavors, improved our recipe, and worked together to create Graham Balls that are colorful, tasty, and enjoyable. Through Graham Bliss, we hope to turn our simple idea into a memorable brand while gaining valuable experience in entrepreneurship, teamwork, branding, and digital marketing.
